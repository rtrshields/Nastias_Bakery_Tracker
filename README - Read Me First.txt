NASTIA'S BAKERY TRACKER — HOW TO USE
=====================================

WHAT THIS IS
This is your own private bakery program — a pantry tracker, a recipe book with
real costs, and a bake log, all in one place. It works on your computer, your
phone, or a tablet. Everything you enter is saved right there on that device;
nothing is sent anywhere or shared with anyone.

HOW TO OPEN IT
Go to this address in any browser (Chrome, Edge, or Safari), on any device:

    https://rtrshields.github.io/Nastias_Bakery_Tracker/

- On your computer: bookmark it so you can get back to it in one click.
- On your phone or tablet: open that link, then look for "Add to Home
  Screen" in your browser's menu. That puts an icon on your home screen so
  it opens like a regular app from now on.

IMPORTANT — HOW YOUR DATA IS SAVED
Your recipes, inventory, and bake history are saved right inside whichever
browser you're using — not on any website or server. That means:

- If you use it on your phone AND your computer, they each keep their OWN
  separate copy of your data — entering a recipe on your phone won't show
  up on your computer by itself.
- To bring them in sync, use Export Backup on one device and Import Backup
  on the other (see below) — you don't need to do this every time, just
  whenever you want your devices to catch up to each other.
- Clearing your browser's history/data, or switching browsers, can erase
  what's saved — so back up regularly (see below) if your data matters to
  you, which it does!

HOW THE PROGRAM WORKS
- HOME: your dashboard. Big buttons let you quickly enter a purchase, add a
  recipe, or log a bake from anywhere.
- INVENTORY: your pantry. Every time you buy ingredients, click
  "Enter Invoice" and type in what you bought, how much, and what you paid.
  You can enter it in whatever unit is on the package (pounds, ounces,
  cups, etc.) — the program converts it automatically.
- RECIPES: your recipe book, organized by category (Dessert, Savoury,
  etc.). Add a recipe and list its ingredients — the cost is calculated
  automatically from your Inventory prices. If an ingredient isn't in stock
  or hasn't been bought yet, you can type in a backup cost instead. You can
  also fill in prep time, bake time, oven temperature, and step-by-step
  instructions — so it reads like a real recipe card, not just a cost sheet.
  Click into any recipe to see its full bake history.
- If you ever change a recipe, the program keeps the old version too — so
  your history always shows exactly what you used at the time.
- BAKE LOG: every time you actually make something, click "Log a Bake."
  This subtracts the ingredients you used from your Inventory and records
  the cost. You can also jot down how it went and what you'd change next
  time — this gets saved permanently with that specific recipe.

KEEPING A BACKUP, AND SYNCING BETWEEN DEVICES
At the top of the program there are two buttons:

- "Export Backup" — saves a copy of all your data to a file. Do this every
  so often, and especially before switching to a new phone or computer.
- "Import Backup" — loads a backup file back in. By default this MERGES it
  with what's already there: anything new in the file gets added, and
  nothing you already have gets lost or duplicated. This is how you keep
  two devices in sync — export from one, import into the other.
  - If you ever genuinely want to wipe a device and start over from one
    specific backup, choose "Replace everything instead" when it asks —
    but note this deletes anything on that device not in the backup file,
    so use it only when you mean to.

That's it — have fun, and happy baking!
