NASTIA'S BAKERY TRACKER — HOW TO USE
=====================================

WHAT THIS IS
This is your own private bakery program. It works completely offline — no
internet needed, nothing is sent anywhere. Everything you enter is saved
right here on this computer.

HOW TO OPEN IT
1. Find the file called "index.html" in this folder.
2. Double-click it. It will open in your web browser (Chrome or Edge) and
   look like a regular program with a menu on the left.
3. To make this easier next time, right-click "index.html" and choose
   "Create shortcut," then drag that shortcut to your Desktop. From now on,
   just double-click the Desktop shortcut to open your program.

IMPORTANT — PLEASE READ
Do NOT move, rename, or delete this folder, and don't move "index.html" out
of it. Your data is saved by your browser and tied to this exact file in
this exact location. If you move the file, your browser may treat it as a
brand new program with no data.

It's also a good idea to keep this whole folder somewhere safe (like your
Documents folder), and NOT inside a folder that automatically empties
itself (like a Downloads folder you regularly clean out).

HOW THE PROGRAM WORKS
- HOME: your dashboard. Big buttons let you quickly enter a purchase, add a
  recipe, or log a bake from anywhere.
- INVENTORY: your pantry. Every time you buy ingredients, click
  "Enter Invoice" and type in what you bought, how much, and what you paid.
  You can enter it in whatever unit is on the package (pounds, ounces,
  cups, etc.) — the program converts it automatically.
- RECIPES: your recipe book, organized by category (Dessert, Savoury,
  etc.). Add a recipe and list its ingredients — the cost is calculated
  automatically from your Inventory prices. If an ingredient isn't in your
  Inventory yet, you can type in an estimated cost instead. Click into any
  recipe to see its full bake history.
- If you ever change a recipe, the program keeps the old version too — so
  your history always shows exactly what you used at the time.
- BAKE LOG: every time you actually make something, click "Log a Bake."
  This subtracts the ingredients you used from your Inventory and records
  the cost. You can also jot down how it went and what you'd change next
  time — this gets saved permanently with that specific recipe.

BACKING UP YOUR DATA (recommended!)
At the top of the program there are two buttons:
- "Export Backup" — saves a copy of all your data to a file you can keep
  somewhere safe (like a USB drive, email to yourself, or a cloud folder).
  Do this every so often, especially after a lot of new entries.
- "Import Backup" — loads a backup file back in, replacing what's
  currently there. Useful if you get a new computer or something goes
  wrong.

That's it — have fun, and happy baking!
